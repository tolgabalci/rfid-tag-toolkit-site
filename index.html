An iPhone app for evaluating UHF RFID inlays and labels with a Zebra RFD40 sled: how far each one reads on the product it goes on, and which one to choose.

  • Measure range where it matters. Test a label on the product, at its spot and in the display it will have. Step away with a tape measure, or walk away while an Apple Watch or a second iPhone beside the tag measures the distance. The app takes short readings and works out how far the label reads reliably and how far it reads at all.
  • Compare inlays. A matrix of every inlay across your setups, power sweeps on the bench, and PDF reports for your clients. Results from different sled models or link profiles are never mixed.
  • Identify any label. Finds the one tag in front of the sled by its TID, even among blank labels that share a factory EPC, and names its chip.
  • Encode tags. Unique test EPCs from the GS1 demonstration range, retail SGTIN-96 from a UPC scanned with the sled or the camera, hex or text.
  • Inventory and locate. Every tag in range, and a search for one of them that ticks faster as you get closer.

Your data stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud when you sync. No account, no tracking.

Works with the Zebra RFD40 Premium and Premium Plus UHF RFID sleds, including the RFD40-M. Zebra and RFD40 are trademarks of Zebra Technologies; this app is not affiliated with Zebra.
