Support

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
  • A Zebra RFD40 Premium or Premium Plus UHF RFID sled, including the RFD40-M. The RFD40 Standard has no Bluetooth, so it can't connect to an iPhone.
  • To measure distances without a tape: an Apple Watch Series 6 or later or an Apple Watch Ultra, with watchOS 26 or later, or a second iPhone, as the beacon beside the tag. The beacon and the iPhone with the sled need Ultra Wideband: iPhone 11 or later, except iPhone SE, 16e and 17e. The Apple Watch SE has no Ultra Wideband either.
  • No sled yet? Turn on Demo mode in Settings to explore with sample evaluations and a simulated sled.

Connecting the sled

  1. Charge and turn on the sled.
  2. Pair it in iOS Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. Close the 123RFID app if it's open: only one app can use the sled at a time.
  4. Open RFID Tag Toolkit and tap Connect on the Reader tab. A new sled with no region set asks for one first: choose the country you're in.

Frequently asked questions

Identify says several tags answered. Move other labels at least an arm's length away and hold the label flat against the front of the sled.

My new labels all have the same EPC. That's normal for blank inlays. The app identifies each label by its TID and can give each sample a unique test EPC.

What are test EPCs? SGTIN-96 numbers from the GS1 demonstration range (company prefix 952), which never collide with real products. The item reference is the inlay number and the serial is the sample number.

What is "reliable range"? The furthest distance where the label read solidly: it answered in at least 80% of the 250 ms slots of a reading (3 seconds at a tape mark, or the best 2 seconds at a mark you walk past). A weaker reading closer in is shown as a weak spot. "Absolute range" is the furthest distance with any read. In the app, Settings → Help explains the whole method.

How do I measure distance without a tape? Use your Apple Watch, with RFID Tag Toolkit open on it, or a second iPhone with the app open at Reader → Distance beacon, as the beacon beside the tag. Choose it once in Settings → Distance beacon and calibrate each sled with a few trigger pulls at tape marks. Then pull the trigger at each mark, or choose Walk in a range test and just walk away from the tag.

How do I keep my data safe? With iCloud sync on, your data stays the same on every iPhone signed in to your Apple Account, and a backup goes to iCloud Drive once a day. Settings → Data → Back up now saves everything, photos included, to Files or AirDrop. Restore from the same screen.

The app closed unexpectedly. On the next launch the app offers to email the diagnostic log to support. It helps a lot with finding the cause.
