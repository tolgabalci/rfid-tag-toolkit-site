Support

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • A Zebra RFD40 UHF RFID sled (RFD40 Standard, Premium or Premium Plus).
  • No reader yet? Turn on Demo mode in Settings to explore with sample data and a simulated reader.

Connecting the RFD40

  1. Charge and turn on the sled.
  2. Pair it in iOS Settings → Bluetooth.
  3. Close the 123RFID app if it's open — only one app can use the reader at a time.
  4. Open RFID Tag Toolkit → Reader tab → Connect.

Frequently asked questions

Identify says several tags answered. Move other labels at least an arm's length away and hold the label flat against the front of the sled.

My new labels all have the same EPC. That's normal for blank inlays. The app identifies each label by its TID and can give each sample a unique test EPC.

What are test EPCs? SGTIN-96 numbers from the GS1 demonstration range (company prefix 952…), which never collide with real products. The item reference is the inlay number and the serial is the sample number.

What is "reliable range"? The last distance before the first reading that wasn't solid (the tag answered in at least 80% of 250 ms slots of a 3-second reading), walking outward from the reader. "Absolute range" is the furthest distance with any read. The in-app help (Settings → How it's measured) explains the full method.

How do I keep my data safe? Settings → Data → Back up now saves everything (including photos) to Files or AirDrop. Restore from the same screen.
