Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

In short: RFID Tag Toolkit does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you record stays on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share it.

Data stored on your device

Inlays, samples, scenarios, measurements, photos, notes, reader settings and a diagnostic log are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. The developer has no access to them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites. It does not use the internet to function.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a label or a tag's placement, or to scan a product barcode. Photos are stored in the app on your device.

Bluetooth and the RFID reader

The app connects to your Zebra RFID reader over Bluetooth to read and write RFID tags you point it at. Tag identifiers and readings are stored on your device as part of your evaluations.

Exports and backups

When you export a report, CSV/JSON file or backup, the iOS share sheet opens and you decide where it goes (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). The developer never receives these files.

Diagnostic log

A small technical log (for example connection events and errors, no measurements or photos) is kept on your device. It leaves the device only if you choose to share it.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
