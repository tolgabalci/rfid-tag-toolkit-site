Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

In short: RFID Tag Toolkit does not collect, sell or share any personal data. What you record stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The developer never sees it.

Data stored on your device

Inlays, samples, scenarios, measurements, photos, notes, reader settings and a diagnostic log are stored in the app's storage on your iPhone. The developer has no access to them. Deleting the app deletes this copy.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites. It uses the internet only for iCloud sync and backups, through your own iCloud account.

iCloud

With iCloud sync on (the default), your evaluations, readings, photos and test groups are stored in your private iCloud database, so they stay the same on your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. With automatic iCloud backup on (also the default), a backup file is saved to your iCloud Drive once a day. PDF reports you create are saved to iCloud Drive too, in RFID Tag Toolkit → Reports. Apple holds this data under your iCloud account; the developer can't access it. You can turn sync and backups off in Settings → Data, and delete reports in Saved Reports.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a label or a tag's placement, or to scan a product barcode. Photos are stored in the app.

Bluetooth and the RFID sled

The app connects to your Zebra RFID sled over Bluetooth to read and write the RFID tags you point it at, and to read product barcodes with the sled's scanner. Tag identifiers and readings are stored as part of your evaluations.

Distance beacon (Nearby Interaction, local network and Apple Watch)

If you choose to measure range test distances automatically, your Apple Watch or a second iPhone running this app is the beacon beside the tag. The iPhone and the beacon exchange a one-time ranging token and measure the distance between them with Ultra Wideband. A second iPhone is found over your local network and Bluetooth, and the two phones talk to each other directly, encrypted. The Apple Watch talks only to the iPhone it is paired with. Nothing is sent to the developer, and no location data is involved.

Exports and backups you share

When you export a report, a CSV or JSON file, or a backup, the iOS share sheet opens and you decide where it goes (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). The developer never receives these files.

Diagnostic log

A small technical log (for example connection events and errors, no measurements or photos) is kept on your device. It leaves the device only if you choose to share it or send it to support.

Emails to support

If you email support, from the app or directly, the developer receives your email address, your message and anything you attach, such as the diagnostic log or the app and device details the app adds to the email. You see all of it before you send. It is used only to answer you and is not shared with anyone.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
